  into a pinch. 10 px is the board's touch slop already (a pan's tap, `PINCH_YOUNG_PX`). The
  watch is on the window in the capture phase, so a cancelled gesture (a pinch's synthetic
  `pointercancel`) ends it too.
- **Select only.** In Draw a held finger on a stroke makes a dot, in Erase a press erases, and
  the Comment tool opens its editor on the press: arming a hold there would change what those
  commit. The right click still works in every tool (TD-131).
- **The menu opens under the finger** (its top-left at the press), as the right click's does;
  the lift doesn't run or close it (the walkthrough holds it open after the lift, nothing
  picked).

## Why two releases

Three of the items change what a log may hold, and a cold load re-validates every logged
transaction. The v10 server (e878c30) rolled back to would refuse:

| Item | New in a log | v10 on replay |
| --- | --- | --- |
| #56 | a scene name of 25–48 characters | `TextTooLong` |
| #59 | `FieldValue::CalloutSide`, `Callout.side` | can't parse the field value; drops a record's `side` |
| #60 | `FieldValue::StrokeColor` | can't parse it |

So release 1 reads them and takes none from outside; release 2 writes them. Recolouring an
**arrow or a comment** needs no reader: `ArrowColor` and `CalloutColor` are field values v10
already takes (no client ever sent one). They ship with #60 in release 2 anyway, so the feature
arrives whole.
