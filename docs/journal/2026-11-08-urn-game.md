# 2026-11-08: The urn drawn as the game draws it

The operator, on the urn the last pass made stand out: "don't make it too big, it looks bigger
than the rift in that screenshot. Can we just match how the real map looks?" Spec §6b (The
Soul Urn) holds the design.

## What the game does

All of it is in `hud_minimap.vcss` and `hud_minimap.vxml`, which the extractor already writes
to every pack (`hud/`). Nothing new had to be extracted.

- **On the ground**, `.map_button.idol_spawn`: 32 × 32 px, `soul_jar_marker` at 100%, and
  `img-shadow: 0 0 18px shardColor&50`. That is the same box as a rift (`.capture_point`,
  32 × 32). The jar fills 60 × 100 of its 128 px art, so it reads smaller than the rift's disc.
- **Carried**, `.held_idol_image` on the player's minimap panel: 32 × 32 px,
  `translateX(8px) translateY(10px)`, `img-shadow: 0 0 8px 0.5 white`, and a 1.3 s pulse
  (brightness 0.75, 1, 0.5; scale 1 to 1.1). It has no team colour.
- **Dropped**: no look of its own. `.idol_dropping` has a grey wash and a 10 px magenta border,
  which reads as debug styling.
- **The open delivery point**, `.idol_return_friendly`: a 96 px box with
  `soul_jar_marker_return` at 50% (48 px) and `img-shadow 8px gray`. Over it sit three
  `soul_jar_marker_return_pulse` images (`.idolReturnTarget`: 48 px, washed `#34ddff`;
  `ColorEnemyObjective` for `_enemy`). Each runs `@keyframes idolPulse` over 1.5 s, 0.35 s
  apart: from 2.35× at opacity 0 to 1× at 0.5 by 60%, held there.
  A closed point's `map_button` stays at opacity 0, so the game hides it.

## What changed

- The ground urn is the game's 32 px marker at the markers' scale, with its shadow. The glow,
  the dark disc, the halo and the ring are gone. A dropped urn looks the same.
- The carried urn is `.held_idol_image`, with no ring and no team colour.
- The delivery point is 48 px: the jar with its gray shadow, and when open, the three
  converging pulses. The brass ring and our wave are gone. A closed point stays dimmed (ours).
  Under reduced motion, one pulse at rest and no held-urn pulse. Sandbox uses the same marker.
- Without a pack, the urn is a cyan jar drawn where the art's opaque part sits, so it has the
  same box.

## Surprises

- **The game sets the hero icon's size in code.** `.map_button.player` is `1%` in the CSS. So
  the held urn takes a hero token's size, and its offset is 8/32 and 10/32 of that size.
- **The game's ground urn is bigger than ours was.** It was 52 view units (19 HUD px) against
  the game's 32 HUD px. Our ring, halo and glow made it look bigger than a rift. The game's jar
  is taller (25 px) but narrow, and fits inside a rift's disc.
