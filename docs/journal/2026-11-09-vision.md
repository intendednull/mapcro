# 2026-11-09: Vision: the map as one team saw it

The operator asked whether Player could do what a replay viewer does: show only the heroes the
chosen team could see at the time. The vision spike (docs/research/replay-vision.md) had found
each team's own minimap feed in the demo; this builds on it.

## What changed

- **Worker, format 4.** `Player.seen`: per hero, `[start_ds, end_ds)` tenths of a second the
  enemy team saw it, from each `CCitadelTeam`'s `m_vecFOWEntities` (`m_bVisibleOnMap` of the
  hero pawn's entry), read every tick, with gaps under 0.5 s merged. Read field by field, never
  through boon's `EnemyMinimap`, which resolves Steam ids. The validator checks the intervals'
  shape. On 129738985: 2,052 intervals, +26,570 bytes (1,501,758 to 1,528,328, +1.8%), the
  spike's numbers; a real-replay test pins them.
- **Player.** A "Vision: All / Hidden King / Archmother" select on the transport. In a team's
  view an enemy out of sight is a ghost at its true position when last seen ("· Seen 0:41 ago"
  on its label, no health ring), and one not seen yet, or not since it died, isn't drawn.
  Local to the browser like Names.
- **Follow-up, same day (operator).** "The urn should not be visible when carried by when the
  carrier isn't visible": in a team's view the urn on a carrier it can't see now isn't drawn
  or hoverable, ghost carrier or undrawn alike. Then: faded tokens "jump around a lot", so out
  of sight now means not drawn by default; a "Show last seen location" toggle beside the
  select (team views only, kept in this browser like Names) brings the ghosts back. The urn
  never follows a ghost, toggle or not.
- **Follow-up, fades (operator).** "Can we make it so the fade in and out? so it's a little
  smoother transition": on continuous playback an enemy entering a team's sight fades in, one
  leaving it stays mounted where last drawn while it fades out (unhoverable, no label), then
  goes; a ghost seen again elsewhere leaves an echo fading on its spot while the hero fades in;
  the carried urn follows its carrier's fade. `board/fade.ts`, Web Animations on opacity.

## Surprises

- **The spike's numbers held.** The worker's merged count is 2,052, one under the spike's 2,053
  (its merge was a separate script; the one interval wasn't chased), and the size is the same
  26.6 KB.
- **The scene ghost was too faint for a real map.** At the scene ghost's 42% the desaturated
  portraits all but vanished on 129738985's terrain; Player's ghosts are at 60%.
- **Ganks read well.** At 30:20 the King's view has Deadman Danny (Sapphire 0) as a ghost last
  seen 41 s earlier near mid. He is in fact beside Amber 5, who dies at 30:23, and only then
  shows.

## Defaults chosen

- A select (like Speed) rather than three buttons: one control's width on a crowded bar.
- A death since the last sighting drops the ghost: the spot no longer means anything, and a
  dead enemy isn't drawn in any view.
- A ghost's health ring isn't drawn: its health out of sight isn't known to the team.
- The token jumps, not glides, from its ghost spot when seen again, as the game's minimap does;
  only the dimming eases.
- Label: the token's own name, then " · Seen m:ss ago", floored to the second.
- Intervals are half-open and rounded to the nearest tenth; a glimpse under a tenth keeps one.
- Out of sight is undrawn by default (operator); the toggle's label is the operator's words,
  on the bar beside the select, hidden in All, where it means nothing.
- The hidden urn is the host's: a carried urn's `at` is `null` in that view, so the urn's
  drawing and hover, which need it, have nothing to show. Its delivery point stays lit as before.
- **Fades: one timing both ways.** 350 ms out-expo, as every eased value. A shorter fade-in was
  weighed for heroes coming into sight, and not needed: out-expo is front-loaded (half way at
  35 ms, 75% at 70 ms, 90% at 117 ms), so an appearing hero reads at once.
- **Fades: only continuous playback.** The host's `continuous` memo on t says whether this
  moment follows the last (same run of the playback clock, same view and toggle, same match,
  under a second of wall time apart); a seek, scrub, jump or stalled tab snaps, so fades never
  pile up across a jump. One fade per element at a time, each starting from where the last left
  it, so a quick out-and-back at 8× never flashes.
- **Fades: switching Vision snaps.** A view switch is the coach's own act, not the match moving:
  the new view shows at once, rather than half the enemy team fading out together as if
  something had happened in the game. Toggling Show last seen location snaps likewise.
- **Fades: only vision's.** A death, a respawn or the end of a carry is drawn as before; the
  host marks an entry `unseen` when vision, and nothing else, hides it.
- **Fades: reduced motion** reads `--value-ease-ms` (0 there) and holds nothing: gone at once.
- **Fades: the ghost's echo is its own element.** A ghost seen again a token's half-width or more
  away fades out on its spot as a copy (`data-echo`, not hoverable), while the live token fades
  in where it is; nearer than that, only the dimming eases, as before.
