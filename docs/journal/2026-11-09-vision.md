# 2026-11-09: Vision: the map as one team saw it

The operator asked whether Player could do what a replay viewer does: show only the heroes the
chosen team could see at the time. The vision spike (docs/research/replay-vision.md) had found
each team's own minimap feed in the demo; this builds on it.

## What changed

- **Worker, format 4.** `Player.seen`: per hero, `[start_ds, end_ds)` tenths of a second the
  enemy team saw it, from each `CCitadelTeam`'s `m_vecFOWEntities` (`m_bVisibleOnMap` of the
  hero pawn's entry), read every tick, with gaps under 0.5 s merged. Read field by field, never
  through boon's `EnemyMinimap`, which resolves Steam ids. The validator checks the intervals'
  shape. On 129738985: 2,052 intervals, +26,570 bytes (1,501,758 to 1,528,328, +1.8%), the
  spike's numbers; a real-replay test pins them.
- **Player.** A "Vision: All / Hidden King / Archmother" select on the transport. In a team's
  view an enemy out of sight is a ghost at its true position when last seen ("· Seen 0:41 ago"
  on its label, no health ring), and one not seen yet, or not since it died, isn't drawn.
  Local to the browser like Names.

## Surprises

- **The spike's numbers held.** The worker's merged count is 2,052, one under the spike's 2,053
  (its merge was a separate script; the one interval wasn't chased), and the size is the same
  26.6 KB.
- **The scene ghost was too faint for a real map.** At the scene ghost's 42% the desaturated
  portraits all but vanished on 129738985's terrain; Player's ghosts are at 60%.
- **Ganks read well.** At 30:20 the King's view has Deadman Danny (Sapphire 0) as a ghost last
  seen 41 s earlier near mid. He is in fact beside Amber 5, who dies at 30:23, and only then
  shows.

## Defaults chosen

- A select (like Speed) rather than three buttons: one control's width on a crowded bar.
- A death since the last sighting drops the ghost: the spot no longer means anything, and a
  dead enemy isn't drawn in any view.
- A ghost's health ring isn't drawn: its health out of sight isn't known to the team.
- The token jumps, not glides, from its ghost spot when seen again, as the game's minimap does;
  only the dimming eases.
- Label: the token's own name, then " · Seen m:ss ago", floored to the second.
- Intervals are half-open and rounded to the nearest tenth; a glimpse under a tenth keeps one.
