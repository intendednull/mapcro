## Defaults chosen

- **Team names.** Checked on deadlock.wiki: Old Gods, New Blood renamed the Amber Hand to the
  Hidden King and the Sapphire Flame to the Archmother.
  - Full names: "Hidden King" / "Archmother".
  - Short forms (`teamShort`, used for placeholders like "King 2"): "King" / "Archmother".
  - Slot tags are K1–K6 / A1–A6. "A" now means Archmother.
  - Wire keys, CSS classes and `data-slot="Amber-0"` are unchanged. Event search still matches
    "amber" / "sapphire".
- **Selection.** A solid ring with a glow in `--me`, my presence colour (set on the map box), on
  both heroes and objectives. Others' objective picks show in their own colour. Camps lost their
  `-40%` ring override and use the same `-14%` as every other marker.
- **Keys.** Q Move, E Draw, R Arrow, T Comment, A Laser, S Erase.
  - The most used tools get the nearest keys.
  - W is unbound, and D is free.
  - F keeps follow-the-presenter, which is why it isn't a tool. K keeps kill/revive.
  - Hover + S (Erase's key, read from `TOOLS`) deletes the thing under the pointer.
- **Portrait-click rule.**
  - Once a slot has a hero, a click on its portrait focuses that hero, for everyone.
  - An editor changes the hero with a small swap button in the portrait's top-left corner (still
    `data-testid="pick-hero"`), the twin of the vision toggle on the right.
  - An editor's empty slot: the portrait is the picker, as before.
  - Souls, ult and vision keep their own controls.
  - Roster chips (K1…) focus too.
- **Focus behaviour.**
  - Zoomed in: a 360 ms ease-out glide to centre the hero, at the same zoom. Any pan or zoom of
    yours cancels it.
  - At the fit view the view stays put.
  - Every focus pings the hero's spot (presence, so others see it) and pulses the token twice.
  - While following someone, your view stays theirs: only the ping and the flash.
  - In Player the position is the match's at t. A hero with no spot there (dead, not in the match)
    gets nothing.
  - On a board, a dead token still stands on the map, so it gets pinged where it lies.
