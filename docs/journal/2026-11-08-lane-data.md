# 2026-11-08: The game's own lane and camp state

Queue items 2 (jungle camps) and the zipline lead from the [Player handoff](2026-11-08-player-handoff.md),
and two of its open questions. Spec §6a/§6b hold the design; this is what doing it taught us.

## What changed

- **Ziplines replace the wave split** (3ae373f, c46a681). The worker reads `CCitadelZipLineNode`
  and writes, per lane, `[second, amber_t, sapphire_t]` where an edge moves (`zips`). Player
  colours each lane from either end to its team's edge and leaves the gap bare. `waves`, the 5 s
  median of trooper fronts, "Waves held" and the stale-split item (queue 4) are gone: the
  colour is captured ground and needs neither. Save scene keeps one split per lane, the gap's
  midpoint.
- **Camps** (same commits). The game rules' timers, read every tick and matched to sites by
  origin, give each camp's first spawn and its cleared intervals (`camps`). Player shows only the
  state; the countdown is a hover (or tap) tooltip. The mid boss comes from its timer too, so
  the replay tier now knows its respawn.
- **Revives** (29a5624). See the deaths question below.
- **Keys** (c46a681). `markerKey` resolves timeline keys to the board's markers. See the patron
  question below.

## Checks on 129738985

- Zipline edges: equal to camp-probe's `zip_edges_t_1hz.csv` in all 2,641 seconds × 3 lanes.
  The map draws what the timeline says: at 20:00 the lanes read 423–430, 574–663 and 341–460,
  as the CSV does, and the drawn path ends sit on those `t`s.
- Camps: all 51 camps' first spawns and clears equal the probe's `camp_timeline.csv`, to the
  rounded second (225 clears; the mid boss 2, at 31:05 and 39:46).
- Sizes: `zips` 53.6 KB and `camps` 5.6 KB, against 31.9 KB for the old `waves`. The timeline
  went from 1.46 MB to 1.49 MB.
- A mutation (the Sapphire part drawn from Amber's edge) fails the e2e lane-length assertion
  (0.67 against 0.3).

## Surprises

- **Every camp timer reads −1 on the recording's first tick**, then its first spawn time on the
  second. Read naively, every camp is up at 0:00 and cleared at once. An "up" shorter than a
  second now counts as nothing. The research CSVs showed it (`init`, then `cleared` at tick 2);
  the derivation missed it on the first try.
- **The dev pack keys sites differently from the built-in map.** Patrons are `lane3`, the mid
  boss `lane4`, camps `camp:neutral:lane{1,4,6}:N`; the built-in map (which the worker uses)
  has `lane0` for all of them. Exact-key lookups silently missed the patron's and mid boss's
  health on pack boards, and would have missed every camp.

## Open questions, answered

- **Patron key.** The board's markers use the **pack's** key when a pack is loaded
  (`patron:sapphire:lane3:1`, the metadata tier's spelling) and the built-in map's
  (`patron:sapphire:lane0:1`) without one. The worker always writes the built-in one. The
  destroyed state already fell back on kind and team, so the patron fell on both tiers. Health,
  camps and event places didn't, and that is what `markerKey` fixes. Unit tests cover both
  directions; the real-pack shots show camps and the mid boss resolving.
- **78 deaths against 74.** The four extra deaths are 3-second gaps in the pawn's life state.
  Each hero came back alive within ~350 units of where it fell (Amber 77 at 42:25, Amber 87 at
  42:19, Sapphire 78 at 32:33, Sapphire 13 at 32:08; the last died for real 6 s later). Real
  respawns on this match take ≥ 10 s and start ≥ 10,000 units away. The metadata doesn't count
  them. They're revives in place (an item or ability; not identified), and no longer count as
  deaths: 74 = 74.

## Left open

- Which revive mechanic it is. The rule is by shape (≤ 5 s, ≤ 2,500 units), not by cause.
- A fully neutral lane (both edges at their ends) draws nothing at all. Real matches never have
  one, since each base owns its end node, but a synthetic timeline shows it.
- The clearing team (CurrencyChanged source 15) isn't read.
