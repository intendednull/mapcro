Tools and gestures

The map is still the surface for every change. A compact picker under the map only chooses what the cursor does, each tool with its own key and cursor. There are no property panels.

Move: drag a hero

The default tool (V). Grab a hero's body or a callout. Everyone watches it move live; dropping saves one change, and one Ctrl+Z puts it back. Drag empty map to pan.

cursor: grabcursor: grabbing
Draw: freehand ink

Draw (D) leaves ink saved with the scene, one stroke per drag. Pick its colour from the palette that opens beside Draw; the Draw button shows the current colour.

cursor: pen
Arrow: drag out a rotation

Arrow (A): drag from anywhere. Starting on a hero gives the arrow that hero's team colour; from empty map it takes the last colour used.

cursor: crosshair
Callout: click to place a label

Callout (C): click where it belongs and type in place. Enter keeps it, Esc removes it. In Move, double-click a callout to edit its text.

cursor: callout
Laser: hold, then fade

Laser (L) draws like ink, but the trail holds for about a second, then fades from its tail. Everyone has it, viewers included. It travels as presence, so it's never saved and never undone.

cursor: laser
Erase, and Clear ink

Erase (E): click or scrub across ink, arrows and callouts. Clear ink, beside the tools, removes every stroke on this scene in one step, and its toast offers Undo. Heroes and objectives can't be erased.
