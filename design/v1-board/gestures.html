The map is the editor

Every change to a scene happens on the map or on the roster itself. There are no tool modes and no property panels: what you grab decides what happens, and the cursor says so before you press.

Drag a hero to move it

Grab the token's body. Everyone watches it move live; dropping it saves one change, and one Ctrl+Z puts it back.

Drag from the rim to draw an arrow

Hovering a hero shows its rim and a small arrow handle. Drag from there, or from empty map, and the arrow takes that hero's team colour.

Click an objective to cycle it

Active, destroyed, hidden, and around again. Hidden objectives show as a faint outline while the pointer is on the map, so they can be found and restored.

Double-click the map for a callout

A callout appears where you clicked, already in edit mode. Type, then Enter keeps it and Esc removes it. Double-click an existing one to edit it.

Right-click a hero for more

A small radial menu around the token: mark dead or alive, swap hero, unplace. On touch, long-press the token. The wedge under the pointer lights up.
