Design notes, v1 board

The heart of mapcro is preparing a run of scenes and flipping through them together. These choices serve that; the questions below are where your call matters most. Pin a comment on any of them.

Decisions

The map is the editor

No tool rail, no modes and no property panels. Drag a hero to move it, drag from its rim to draw an arrow, click an objective to cycle it, double-click for a callout, right-click for the rest. One line of hints under the map is the only tool chrome.

Scenes are a film strip, not a menu

The strip is always on screen along the bottom, with a mini map of each scene and the game clock set large. Flipping is the main action, so it gets the most room and the arrow keys.

The frame says whether you're live

The brass map frame glows spirit-green while you present or follow, and goes dashed and dim when you've drifted. You can read your follow state without reading any text.

Presence sits on the scenes

Avatars ride on the scene card each person is looking at, so a presenter sees at a glance who is with them and who wandered off to scene 5.

Drifting is gentle; resuming is one key

Panning, zooming or flipping on your own pauses follow without a prompt. The banner turns into “Resume following (F)”, and the presenter's scene card gets a dashed outline in their colour.

Game clock is the scene's name

Scene titles start with the in-game time (“8:00 · mid boss setup”), and the clock is the typographic anchor in the strip and the title.

A link is a key, and the dialog says so

Share is owner-only. Every link has a label, a role and a live-use count. Revoking one asks once and names who gets disconnected.

Honest save status

Saved, Syncing (n), Offline: n kept, Rejected. Offline work that can't merge becomes a named offline copy, never a silent drop.

Original placeholder art only

The map is an original schematic: three lanes, a mid, two bases and generic glyphs. Heroes are team-coloured tokens with initials. A server's extracted pack replaces the art; none of it lives in the repo.

Open questions

Q1
Can the presenter pull people back?

Today follow is opt-in per viewer (spec §8). Should the presenter get a “Bring everyone back” nudge that re-offers follow to people who drifted, or is the dashed card on the strip enough?

Q2
Does flipping scenes as a follower end follow, or only pause it?

This design pauses: you look around, and one key snaps you back to wherever the presenter is now. The alternative is a hard stop that needs an explicit “Follow Coach” again.

Q3
What does an edit-link holder see instead of Share?

Share is owner-only, so editors and viewers currently get no Share button at all. Should they get “Ask the owner for a link”, or nothing?

Q4
Drag on empty map: arrow, or pan?

This design makes a drag on empty map draw an arrow, so panning needs Space+drag, a right-drag or two fingers. Coaches pan a lot while presenting. Should empty-map drag pan instead, with arrows only from a hero's rim?

Q5
Which typeface?

These boards use system fonts only, so nothing loads from the network. The intent is a self-hosted OFL face with deco-roman proportions for clocks and titles (candidates: Marcellus, Federo, Poiret One) and a plain humanist sans for the UI. Do you want a face in the repo, or stay on system fonts?
